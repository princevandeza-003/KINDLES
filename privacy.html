Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Kindle (“we”, “us”) is operated by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: [your-email@example.com].

Who can use Kindle

Kindle is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we delete such accounts when we find them.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (the password is handled by our authentication provider and is not readable by us).
  • Profile: first name, birthdate, gender, who you are interested in, what you are looking for, your prompt answer, interests, and photos you choose to upload.
  • Location (optional): if you tap “Use my location”, we store your position rounded to about 1 km. Other users only see an approximate distance, never your location. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Photos: only images you pick from your gallery. We do not read the rest of your gallery.
  • Microphone (optional): only while you record a voice message in a chat. Voice messages are stored privately and can only be played by the two people in that chat.
  • Activity: your likes, passes and super likes, your matches, your messages, the people you block, and reports you send. Messages also record when they were read and any emoji reactions, which the other person in the chat can see. Your answer to the daily question is visible to people who can see your profile.
  • Verification selfie (optional): if you ask for a verified badge, a selfie we use only to compare with your photos. It is stored privately and deleted after review.

How we use it

To run the app: show your profile to people who fit what they are looking for (and you fit what they are looking for), calculate compatibility and distance, deliver messages, keep the community safe, and respond to reports. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

What other users see

People you could match with can see your name, age, photos, gender, what you are looking for, prompt answer, interests and approximate distance. Matches can also see your messages to them. People who are looking for someone like you can also find you by searching your first name.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase provides our database, login and file storage.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app’s web files and may log technical data such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts may receive your IP address when the app loads its fonts.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety.

Keeping your data

We keep your data until you delete your account. In the app, go to My profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, matches, messages, location and voice messages. You can also request deletion by emailing [your-email@example.com] or see how to delete your account. Backups may take a short time to clear.

Your rights

You can view and edit most of your data in the app. You can ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing [your-email@example.com]. Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights under your local data protection law, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so users can only reach what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

International transfers

Our providers may store data on servers outside your country.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
