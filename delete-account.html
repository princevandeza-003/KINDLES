Delete your Kindle account

Effective date: November 10, 2026

You can delete your Kindle account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Kindle and log in.
  2. Go to My profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and type DELETE to confirm.

This permanently removes your profile, photos, location, matches, messages, voice messages, and your login. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot log in

Email [princevandeza@gmail.com] from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm and complete the deletion.

See our Privacy Policy for details on the data we keep.
